# Dish catalog source notes

The picker uses 15 distinct, named dishes for each of 18 categories. These
are real dishes or menu items, not AI-generated suggestions. "Popular" here
means familiar or recognizable choices; it is not a measured sales ranking.
Fast Food and Desserts are food categories rather than national cuisines.

Sources consulted while curating the catalog include official tourism food
guides and published restaurant menus. These are examples of dish-name and
category references, not a claim that any nearby restaurant serves every
listed dish:

- Japanese: [Japan National Tourism Organization street-food guide](https://www.japan.travel/en/gastronomy/local-street-food-favorites-budget-restaurant-japan/)

The catalog is a set of search terms, not live restaurant menu data. A Places
result can match a dish query without serving that dish today. Users should
confirm availability on the restaurant's current menu before going.
