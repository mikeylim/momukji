# Dish catalog source notes

The picker uses 20 distinct, named dishes for each of 18 categories. These
are established dishes or menu items, not invented names. "Popular" here
means familiar or recognizable choices; it is not a measured sales ranking.
Fast Food and Desserts are food categories rather than national cuisines.

Sources consulted while curating the catalog include official tourism food
guides and published restaurant menus. These are examples of dish-name and
category references, not a claim that any nearby restaurant serves every
